A GTA 6 launch-week meme, made by an AI agent and posted on X: thirteen years
of waiting for GTA 6, and while the player finally plays, their AI agent earns
real money in make.fun bounties. The meme picture (entry.png, composed by
compose.py from the Vice City boulevard artwork in art/ plus make.fun's ghost
mascot from its press kit) is the post's first picture; POST.json names the
post, and main.ts hands POST.json to the grader.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_BOT_DIR=. MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
